## About me


I am a final-year Biomedical Engineering student at the Institut Supérieur des Technologies Médicales de Tunis, University of Tunis El Manar, specializing in Technology and Information Processing for Health.


My work spans clinical prediction, medical imaging, bioinformatics, and public-health intelligence. I enjoy turning research questions into reproducible pipelines and usable applications.


- **Healthcare AI:** machine learning for clinical data and longitudinal disease monitoring.
- **Medical imaging:** MRI segmentation, radiomics, and glioblastoma follow-up.
- **Public-health intelligence:** multilingual NLP, semantic search, and retrieval-augmented generation.
- **Digital health:** healthcare information systems, APIs, and connected medical devices.
